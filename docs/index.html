Your whole retro library, straight from your NAS

Mirmire is a launcher for Android handhelds, phones and TVs. It plays games from your network storage, keeps your saves in sync and sets up RetroArch for you.

Download for Android 🎨 Try Theme Studio

Free · Android 8.0 or later · Works with RetroArch and standalone emulators

What makes it different

Built around the library you already have

Most launchers expect your games on the device and leave emulators, saves and BIOS files to you. Mirmire is built for a library on your storage, and takes care of the rest.

📡

Your NAS is the library

Browse thousands of games over SMB, SFTP, WebDAV or FTP. A game downloads when you press Play, and big unplayed games are cleared to free space.

🔄

Continue anywhere

Saves and save states sync with your storage. Pick up another device and carry on from the same moment. Devices without shared storage pair over Wi-Fi.

⚙️

RetroArch, set up for you

Cores, hotkeys, auto-resume and a sharp picture, with settings for all games, one console or one game, and suggestions for your device.

🧹

A clean library

One version of each game by your preferred regions. Betas and demos are hidden, and files are checked against No-Intro.

🧩

BIOS checker

See what each console needs and what's missing or the wrong version, then copy the right files from your storage.

✈️

Pack for a trip

Take games offline and play anywhere. Everything syncs back when you're home, and offloading a game keeps its saves.

💡

Stick lights that react

On Retroid and AYN handhelds the lights follow the theme, the battery, the console, the cover art or the performance mode.

👨‍👩‍👧

Profiles for the family

Each player has their own saves, favorites and play time, with PINs and a content filter for children.

💾

Backups that travel

Back up settings, themes and RetroArch's own settings to your storage, restore on any device, or keep devices in sync.

Continue on another device

Start on your phone, finish on your handheld

When another of your devices played a game more recently, the home screen offers to continue it, with that device's save and save state.

  • No account or sign-up
  • Devices sharing storage need no setup
  • Others pair once over Wi-Fi with a 6-digit code

RetroArch

Tuned without opening RetroArch's menus

Pick settings for every game, a whole console or a single game. Mirmire applies them before each launch, and suggests what suits your device.

  • Cores downloaded for you
  • Shaders, hotkeys and auto-resume
  • Your RetroArch settings are included in backups

Library cleanup

One copy of each game, verified

Big collections often have several versions of every game. Mirmire shows the best one for your regions, hides betas and demos, and checks files against No-Intro.

  • Your region order, with fallbacks
  • Games you've played always stay
  • A filter shows anything that failed the check

Spotlight

Something to play, every day

The RetroAchievements weekly challenge, games you're close to mastering, quick and multiplayer picks, and your play time with battery estimates.

Get started

Playing in a few minutes

The setup wizard walks you through everything on first launch.

Install Mirmire

Download the APK from GitHub Releases and open it. Mirmire keeps itself up to date after that.

Add your library

Point it at your NAS share or a folder on the device. Artwork and game details load automatically.

Press Play

Mirmire picks an emulator, sets up RetroArch and downloads the game. Your saves sync from then on.
