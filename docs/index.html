Your whole retro library, straight from your NAS

What makes it different

Built around the library you already have

Most launchers expect your games on the device and leave emulators, saves and BIOS files to you. Mirmire is built for a library on your storage, and takes care of the rest.

Storage and library

Your NAS is the library

Browse thousands of games over SMB, SFTP, WebDAV, FTP or straight from RomM. Away from home it keeps working through Tailscale. A game downloads when you press Play, big unplayed games are cleared to free space, and games only on this device upload to the NAS in one tap.

Works with RomM

Already run RomM? Pair Mirmire with it by scanning its QR code or approving a code: its platforms, covers and details come straight over, games download when you press Play, and saves and states sync to the game's page in RomM.

Drive & Dropbox via rclone

Add an rclone library and point Mirmire at rclone serve webdav on a PC or NAS: Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive and more.

A clean library

One version of each game by your preferred regions. Betas and demos are hidden, and files are checked against No-Intro.

Bring what you already have

Artwork ES-DE, RetroArch or LaunchBox already downloaded shows up straight away. Favorites, play time and collections come over from Pegasus and LaunchBox, and RetroArch's saves, states, settings and BIOS files with one tap.

Pack for a trip

Take games offline and play anywhere. Everything syncs back when you're home, and offloading a game keeps its saves.

Saves and sync

Continue anywhere

Saves and save states sync with your storage. Pick up another device and carry on from the same moment. Devices without shared storage pair over Wi-Fi or sync through Syncthing.

Syncthing for saves

Sync saves straight between your devices through a Syncthing folder, no NAS needed. Its conflict copies turn into Mirmire's usual question: which save to keep.

Backups that travel

Back up settings, themes and RetroArch's own settings to your storage, restore on any device, or keep devices in sync.

Emulators

RetroArch, set up for you

Cores, hotkeys, auto-resume and a sharp picture, with upscaling matched to your screen, settings for all games, one console or one game, and suggestions for your device. SwanStation for PS1; Beetle Saturn when you want accuracy.

Standalone defaults that fit

Automatic prefers DuckStation for PS1, Mupen64Plus FZ for N64, WatermelonDS for DS and ARMSX2 for PS2 when those apps are installed.

Xbox, PlayStation and Steam

Xbox in hakuX, Xbox 360 in Xendroid Plus, PS2 in ARMSX2, PS3 in ARMSX3 or aPS3e, and Steam games from your DroidDeck library. Emulators that aren't on Google Play install from Mirmire in one tap.

BIOS checker

Every file each emulator needs, checked by checksum, with the exact folder it belongs in. Games tell you before you press Play if their console is missing one.

Faster GPU drivers

On Snapdragon devices, Mirmire finds your Adreno GPU, suggests the latest custom driver for it and shows how to load it in your emulator.

Stream your PC games

Games from Moonlight, Artemis or Steam Link get their own Streaming console. Press Play and the stream opens; a game that's also in your Steam library shows once, with a Stream button.

Your device, your players

Stick lights that react

On Retroid and AYN handhelds the lights follow the theme, the battery, the console, the cover art or the performance mode.

Profiles for the family

Each player has their own saves, favorites, play time and a pixel-art avatar. Child profiles get age ratings, daily play time and a parent PIN.

From the couch to the plane

A home page, a TV mode, and saves that follow you

Things you won't find in other retro launchers: a Home page you build from widgets, a full-screen mode for the TV, both screens of a dual-screen handheld, and saves that move with you.

Home and TV

A Home page you build

Clock, weather, calendar, your last game, achievements, play time and music from your storage, plus widgets from your other apps. Drag and resize them by touch, as on a phone, or arrange them with a controller.

Widget plugins

Players make widgets, like a game of the day, and share them as a ZIP. Pixel Pal comes with hundreds of pals from OpenPets that eat, play, nap and cheer your achievements. You see what each one may do before you install it, and turn any of them off in Settings › Plugins.

Big Picture, docked or not

A full-screen home for TVs and controllers, with big cards for consoles and games. Plug into a TV or dock and it switches on by itself; unplug and your layout comes back. Or make it 3D with Mirmire Cartouche.

Two screens, both used

On the AYN Thor, AYANEO Pocket DS or a Retroid Dual Screen add-on, the second screen shows the selected game's art, details, manual and map, or your widgets. Swap them if you like the menu below.

Your launcher, if you like

Set Mirmire as the home app on a handheld or TV box, with your other apps one press away.

While you play

Saves upload when you sleep

Press power in the middle of a game and its saves go to your storage, so your other device offers to continue.

Play together

Invite a paired device on the same Wi-Fi to a netplay game. Your device starts hosting by itself; theirs opens the same game.

Learns how hot games run

A battery, standard or performance mode for each console, stepping down for games that ran hot. You can turn it off.

A pal that helps you play

Turn on AI and Pixel Pal shares tips about the game you just played, chats when you're stuck, and can float over the game. Use a free cloud AI with your own key, or a model on the device that works offline.

RetroAchievements everywhere

Progress on every game card, the weekly challenge, games close to mastery, and your points and streak at a glance.

Home Assistant

Dim the lights when a game starts on the TV, or run any scene or script when you play, stop or step away.

Sync & continue

Start on your phone, finish on your handheld

Saves, save states and favorites sync with your storage. When another of your devices played a game more recently, the home screen offers to continue it, with that device's save and save state.

  • No account or sign-up
  • Devices sharing storage need no setup
  • Others pair once over Wi-Fi with a 6-digit code, or use Syncthing
  • Played on two devices offline? Pick the save to keep, or keep both

RetroArch

Tuned without opening RetroArch's menus

Pick settings for every game, a whole console or a single game. Mirmire applies them before each launch, and suggests what suits your device.

  • Cores downloaded for you
  • Shaders and FSR, ScaleFX or AI upscaling
  • Hotkeys and auto-resume
  • Your RetroArch settings are included in backups

Library cleanup

One copy of each game, verified

Big collections often have several versions of every game. Mirmire shows the best one for your regions, hides betas and demos, and checks files against No-Intro.

  • Your region order, with fallbacks
  • Games you've played always stay
  • A filter shows anything that failed the check
  • BIOS files checked by checksum, per emulator

Parental controls

Games that fit their age

Turn a player into a child profile and Mirmire shows only games rated for their age, using ESRB, PEGI and CERO ratings from ScreenScraper, RomM and LaunchBox.

  • Daily play time, or different limits on some days
  • Allow or block single games
  • A parent PIN guards it all
  • A running game is never cut off

Spotlight

Something to play, every day

The RetroAchievements weekly challenge, games you're close to mastering, quick and multiplayer picks, and your play time with battery estimates.

Home page

A home screen of your own

Turn on the Home page and build it from widgets: the clock, weather in °C or °F, your next events, the game you were playing, achievements, and widgets from your other apps.

  • Drag and resize by touch, or arrange with a controller
  • Plugins shared as a ZIP, like Pixel Pal
  • Hundreds of pals from OpenPets
  • Optional AI: your pal gives game tips and chats, even over a game

Pixel Pal & AI

A companion that knows what you played

Turn on AI hints and your pal welcomes you back with a word about the game you just played, shares tips, and chats while you play, right over the game.

  • Free cloud keys, your own server, or a model on the device
  • AI lines are tagged so you can tell them apart
  • Only game titles, consoles and what you type are sent

Dual screen

Two screens, both put to work

On the AYN Thor, AYANEO Pocket DS or a Retroid with the Dual Screen add-on, the second screen shows the game you've selected: its cover, description and achievements. Or put your widgets there.

  • The controller always works the menu
  • Swap the screens to put the menu below
  • DS and 3DS games get the second screen to themselves

Big Picture

Made for the TV

A full-screen home with big cards for your consoles and games, and the time, battery and achievements on a small bar. Plug into a TV or dock and it switches on by itself.

Big Picture in 3D

Your consoles in 3D, your games on their discs

Install Mirmire Cartouche, built on Cartouche by Hakimba, and Big Picture becomes a 3D home: 3D consoles, and your games as their real discs, cartridges and UMDs with your box art on the labels. Mirmire still plays every game.

  • Free and open source, installed from Settings › Plugins
  • Pick it in Settings › Layout › Big Picture style, or in setup's 3D Big Picture step
  • Touch or controller: swipe, tap, turn the models

Theme Studio

Design your own look, in your browser

Build a theme, a console icon pack or a Home page widget and see it on Mirmire's real screens as you go. Nothing to install, and nothing you add leaves your browser.

Screenshots

Made for controllers, works with touch

Get started

Playing in a few minutes

The setup wizard walks you through everything on first launch.

Install Mirmire

Download the APK from GitHub Releases and open it. Mirmire keeps itself up to date after that.

Add your library

Point it at your NAS, your RomM server, a folder on the device, or rclone for Drive and Dropbox. Coming from Pegasus or LaunchBox? Setup brings your favorites and play time along.

Press Play

Mirmire picks an emulator, sets up RetroArch and downloads the game. Your saves sync from then on.
